Common worries

I’m not a computer person. Can I still do this?

Yes. Copy your GorillaDesk API key, open Connect, paste it once, then copy the snippet into Claude, Cursor, or Codex. After that, you only ask business questions.

Can this mess up my customers or schedule?

No. It can look up customers, invoices, jobs, payments, and techs. It cannot create customers, change jobs, or take payments.

Where do my GorillaDesk credentials go?

When you use Connect, we verify the API key with GorillaDesk, encrypt it, and give you a connect token for your AI app. Treat that token like a password. Don’t paste the GorillaDesk API key into chat.

Where do I get my API key?

In GorillaDesk, open Addons → API, generate a company key, and paste it into Connect once. After that, you just ask questions.

What if I get stuck?

In Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor, paste:

I’m stuck setting up Ask GorillaDesk. I’m not a developer. Tell me the next single step.
Is this made by GorillaDesk?

No. It’s an independent helper that talks to GorillaDesk using their normal API. Follow GorillaDesk’s rules for your account.
