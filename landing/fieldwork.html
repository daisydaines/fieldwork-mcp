    Copy your Fieldwork key

    Fieldwork gives you a private key after you sign in. Treat it like a password.

    1. Open Fieldwork’s key page (or open their API help article and click base).
    2. Log in with your Fieldwork username and password.
    3. Copy the private key it shows you. Don’t text it to anyone.

    Or inside the Fieldwork app: Settings → Users & Routes → Integration.

Common worries

I’m not a computer person. Can I still do this?

Yes. Copy your Fieldwork key, open Connect, paste it once, then copy the snippet into Claude, Cursor, or Codex. After that, you only ask business questions.

Can this mess up my customers or schedule?

No. It can look up customers, money, jobs, chemicals, routes, and techs. It cannot create customers, cancel jobs, or take payments.

Where does my Fieldwork key go?

When you use Connect, we verify the key with Fieldwork, encrypt it, and give you a connect token for your AI app. Treat that token like a password. Don’t paste the Fieldwork key into chat.

Why do I have to copy a key at all?

That’s how Fieldwork lets outside tools look at your account. You copy it once from Settings. After that, you just ask questions.

What if I get stuck?

In Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor, paste:

I’m stuck setting up Ask Fieldwork. I’m not a developer. Tell me the next single step.
Is this made by Fieldwork?

No. It’s an independent helper that talks to Fieldwork using their normal account tools. Follow Fieldwork’s rules for your account.
