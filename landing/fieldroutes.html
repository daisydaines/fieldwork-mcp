Common worries

I’m not a computer person. Can I still do this?

Yes. Copy your FieldRoutes key and token, open Connect, paste them once, then copy the snippet into Claude, Cursor, or Codex. After that, you only ask business questions.

Can this mess up my customers or schedule?

No. It can look up customers, balances, appointments, routes, and techs. It cannot create customers, cancel appointments, or take payments.

Where do my FieldRoutes credentials go?

When you use Connect, we verify key + token with FieldRoutes, encrypt them, and give you a connect token for your AI app. Treat that token like a password. Don’t paste the FieldRoutes key or token into chat.

Why do I need both a key and a token?

That’s how FieldRoutes authenticates outside tools. Both come from Settings → API. You paste them once. After that, you just ask questions.

What if I get stuck?

In Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor, paste:

I’m stuck setting up Ask FieldRoutes. I’m not a developer. Tell me the next single step.
Is this made by FieldRoutes?

No. It’s an independent helper that talks to FieldRoutes using their normal API. Follow FieldRoutes’ rules for your account.
